Independent, experience-led, technology & services advisory for companies in the Lloyd’s & London Specialty Insurance Market.

Mojio & Co provides independent, experience-led advisory services to technology and services providers in the Lloyd's and London Specialty Insurance Market. We help companies seeking to better understand market dynamics, identify emerging opportunities, and help design technology adoption and go-to-market strategies that resonate with Managing Agents, insurers, brokers, and MGAs.

Mojio & Co is your knowledgeable Market guide, a catalyst that leaves you stronger and more independent than before.

Clients typically engage us to help them focus on one or more of three areas: market insight, proposition shaping, and commercial and marketing strategy. Clients have also asked for execution support where we can add tangible value. Further capbilities include assistance with sales leadership and hire selection, as well as vendor and system evaluation.

Mojio & Co actively supports early-stage technology ventures along with more established firms in the Market, including firms considering applying for, or already on, their journey through the Lloyd's Lab. This support reflects our commitment to strengthening the quality and effectiveness of innovation entering the market.

Standardized data exchange plays a key role in improving access to the market, simplifying processes, and ensuring the industry is ready for the future.
